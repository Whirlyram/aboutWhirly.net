STICK TO THE SCRIPT

Directly inspired by the rescue p.AI.nter ending from Pressure

And as you lay there, limp with blood pooling from your chest and your heart held beating in his hand, the same thought mirrors between you two:


Yours?
"Why am I still alive?".

His?
"Why are they still alive?!".

Almost as if to answer the question, the tune of a distorted gramophone begins echoing from nowhere, yet everywhere at once.

The realization hits him hard, further clouding his already broken psyche with madness as he forces his eyes shut.

He rakes a deep gash in the cold steel floor with ease before pounding it in rage. This was the moment he had been oh-so eagerly waiting for, getting to watch the life slowly fade away from the eyes of the person who took away all that he had left: the solace in companionship.

He shoots up, turning to face the music, yelling out in a fury, only to be atomized, reduced to a green mist — a green that strikes you as unmistakeably familiar...


And with that, your vision finally fades to black as you perish...





Your story will not end here.

The pieces will be put back in place.

You will stick to the script this time, for you serve a purpose far greater than you realize..
