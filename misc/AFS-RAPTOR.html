The 'AFS-RAPTOR'

Agent Whirlwind's Main Weapon

The AFS-RAPTOR is a heavily modified hybrid between a SPAS-12 and a DP-12.

It is tube-fed, with each barrel capable of holding 6 shells at once, for a combined total of 12 shells.

The left tube is to be loaded with slug rounds, and the right is to be loaded with standard buckshot, allowing it to function as both a long and short ranged weapon.


It has three firing modes:


LR - Long Range, fires the left barrel which stores long range slugs

CQ - Close Quarters, fires the right barrel which stores 12 gauge buckshot shells

OK - Over Kill, fires both barrels at the same time. NOT for sustained use.

Svampslag is an exceedingly rare foliated metamorphic rock found only at the most active tectonic fault of the Knipovich Ridge.

While only the outermost layer of Svampslag remains harvestable, an in-depth anaylsis has revealed that the layer of svampslag closest to the Earth's core is capable of abosorbing abproximately 2.415 megatons of energy while remaining completely intact.

However, it is not a priority on Urbanshade's radar, as even with their technology, it remains too dangerous to reliably extract, and is estimated to make up only 0.024% of the Knipovich Ridge's fault zone.

For reference roughly 3.814% of the Knipovich Ridge's floor contains Svampslag.
