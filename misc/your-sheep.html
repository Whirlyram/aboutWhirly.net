Z-277, Codename "Your Sheep"

A silly little critter I made to explain the biometric lock thing of my Pressure AU

A tripedal sheep-like critter with a very unique layer of 'wool'. For reasons not yet known, the wool of a Z-277 instance will resonate when in contact with the living tissue of the first thing it imprinted on. This resonance is exploited in the use of cheap – and highly effective – biometric locks. They have a rapid gestative period, allowing the mass production of uniquely imprinted wool to be easy. Interestingly, even if shown a photo of an individual, if that was the first thing they imprinted on, the wool resonates not when touching the photo, but when touching the living tissue of said individual, even though they were not physically there. Because it requires living tissue, not even the trick of using a severed hand would work. Eerily, the wool of a Z-277 instance still functions even if its host is deceased or even obliterated. A pregnant female Z-277 instance is typically ready to give birth within 8 days. It has been noted that should a Z-277 somehow imprint on itself, it will immediately combust. This was discovered after Researcher Shirly Temple suggested the placement of a newly born instance within a mirror room.
It has also been reported that their mutton tastes like 'stale sponges', which is unsurprising considering it lacks significant caloric energy and protein
