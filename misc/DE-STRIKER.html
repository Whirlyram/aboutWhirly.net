The 'DE-STRIKER'

Agent Whirlwind's Sidearm

The DE-STRIKER is a heavily modified Rhino chiappa with twin-drums, allowing it to hold 18 bullets at once.

Due to the variety of threats encountered within the Hadal Blacksite, the Weapons Division has engineered several various types of ammunition to be used by the DE-STRIKER, as well as all other magnum-based firearms.


▶ .357

The standard JHP round for Urbanshade's .357-caliber weaponry.

▶ Less Lethal Round

A standard, less-than-lethal round for use in securing valuable assets without risking (great) bodily harm.

A needle is settled behind the rubber tip of the bullet, allowing it to pierce through the rubber and into the target upon impact.

The needle is laced with Neocytiphite, a fast-acting sedatative capable of incapacitation within 40 seconds after administration.
